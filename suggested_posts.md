Early intervention can prevent academic difficulties from becoming overwhelming as examinations approach. Close supervision and monitoring are equally important. Teachers should be alert to lateness, absenteeism, unnecessary movement, noise, bullying, and other forms of misconduct. Minor disciplinary problems should be addressed promptly before they develop into serious issues. At the same time, discipline should be corrective rather than humiliating, helping learners understand the importance of responsible behaviour. Teachers can encourage them by recognising improvement, setting achievable targets, and reminding them of the importance of consistent effort. Involving parents and guardians can further strengthen the management of the candidate class. Parents should be informed about persistent absenteeism, poor performance, or behavioural concerns.
