The incident has generated widespread media attention in China. An editorial in the People's Daily wrote: “The wholesalers focused solely on their immediate economic interests while completely disregarding the lives and health of consumers. [They] crossed a legal red line and breached the moral bottom line.” China produces well over 30 million tonnes of cabbages each year, nearly half of the world's annual total. Vietnam, South Korea and Russia are among the largest export destinations for the vegetable. Exposure to relatively high quantities of formaldehyde in some workplaces has been linked to some types of cancer in humans, but the effect of exposure to small amounts is less clear, according to the American Cancer Society.

**32. Relief for Taxpayers as Tribunal Rules on Using VAT Credits to Clear Tax Bills**

The ruling arose between a particular company and KRA in which the company had an outstanding VAT liability of Ksh12.86 million while holding an available VAT credit of Ksh19.71 million. The company asked the taxman to have the outstanding VAT liability offset using its available credit under Section 47 of the Tax Procedures Act. However, KRA declined to process the request and proceeded with enforcement action, including issuing agency notices to the company's bank accounts. According to the authority, the Ksh19.71 million constituted excess input VAT governed by Section 17 (5) of the VAT Act, which provides for excess input tax to be carried forward to the next tax period. KRA declined to use the VAT credit to offset the liabilities, maintaining that the credit could not simply be used to offset the company's outstanding liability. The authority's decision was challenged before the Tribunal, with the company arguing that it had sufficient VAT credit to settle the outstanding tax fully and that KRA should have considered its set-off application before taking enforcement action. In its ruling delivered on July 27, 2026, the Tribunal rejected KRA's interpretation. According to the Tribunal, Section 47 of the Tax Procedures Act gives the Commissioner statutory power to apply an available tax credit towards an existing tax liability where circumstances justify the set-off. "The Tribunal therefore finds no reason for the Respondent to resort to enforcement mechanism while it had within its reach available VAT credit nor did it substantiate why denying the Applicant a Tax Compliance Certificate was justified," the Tribunal ruled. It further found that Section 17(5) of the VAT Act does not prohibit KRA from considering a taxpayer's application for a set-off under Section 47 of the Tax Procedures Act. KRA was also ordered to issue the company with a Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) after giving effect to the set-off.

**11. Tribunal ruling opens new path for taxpayers to offset VAT credits against tax debts**

NAIROBI, Kenya, Aug 24 – Kenyan taxpayers with outstanding tax liabilities could get relief after the Tax Appeals Tribunal ruled that the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) must first consider requests to offset tax debts using available VAT credits before taking enforcement action. The ruling stems from a dispute between Easton Petroleum Limited and KRA over a VAT liability of Sh12.86 million. Easton had an available VAT credit of Sh19.71 million and applied on May 14, 2026, to use the credit to settle the outstanding tax. However, KRA did not process the request and instead issued agency notices to freeze the company's bank accounts at KCB and Absa. KRA argued that the VAT credit was excess input tax under the VAT Act and should be carried forward to future tax periods rather than used to settle the debt. The Tribunal disagreed, saying Section 47 of the Tax Procedures Act gives the Commissioner the authority to apply available tax credits to existing tax liabilities where appropriate. “Section 47 of the TPA vests the Commissioner with the statutory power to apply available tax credit towards an existing tax liability where the circumstances justify such an off-set,” the Tribunal ruled. The Tribunal also said the VAT Act's requirement to carry forward excess input tax does not stop KRA from considering a taxpayer's formal request for an offset. It noted that KRA did not dispute Easton's VAT credit or show that its application was defective. “Where the taxpayer has disclosed available tax credit that is sufficient to extinguish an admitted tax liability and has formally sought its application through statutory mechanisms, the Commissioner is under obligation to fairly and reasonably determine the request before resorting to enforcement measures,” the Tribunal said. The decision could benefit businesses that owe VAT but also have VAT credits held by KRA, helping them preserve cash flow instead of waiting for refunds or carrying credits forward. However, the Tribunal clarified that the ruling does not create an automatic right to offset every tax debt against a tax credit.

Mama Ida alitoa kauli hiyo Jumatatu alipokuwa akiadhimisha miaka 76 ya kuzaliwa katika makazi yake Karen, Nairobi, sherehe iliyohudhuriwa na mamia ya wakazi wa Kibra. Kwa mara ya kwanza, Mama Ida alizungumza wazi kuhusu nia ya mwanawe kuingia katika siasa, akisema ameamua “kumwachilia” kwa wakazi wa Kibra na viongozi wa Nairobi waliowahi kufanya kazi na Raila. “Nyinyi watu wa Kibra mmeamua na sina la kufanya. Namwachilia mwanangu mikononi mwenu. Viongozi mlio hapa, mtunzeni, mumfundishe na mumwelekeze. Wengi wenu mlianza siasa na Baba. Mwonyesheni njia,” akasema Bi Ida. Alisema Raila Junior anafaa kuendesha siasa safi bila matusi na vurugu, akisisitiza kuwa Kibra, ambayo ilijulikana kama “chumba cha kulala cha Baba”, ilikuwa nguzo muhimu katika safari ya kisiasa ya Raila. Mama Ida pia aliwataka wakazi wa Kibra kudumisha amani na umoja licha ya tofauti zao za kisiasa. Alisema hataweza kufanya kampeni kwa sababu ya wadhifa wake kama Balozi wa Kenya na Mwakilishi wa Kudumu katika Shirika la Mazingira la Umoja wa Mataifa (UNEP), lakini aliwataka wakazi kupendana na kushirikiana. Raila Junior alisema aliamua kuwania Kibra baada ya kushauriana na wakazi na kwamba anafuata nyayo za babake ambaye alikuwa akishirikiana kwa karibu na wananchi wa kawaida. Alisema siasa za babake zilijikita katika kushirikisha wananchi mashinani, kusaidia wanawake na watu wenye mahitaji. “Nilikuwa uso wa Baba Kibra na nilimsaidia kutekeleza yale aliyoanzisha,” akasema Junior. Ingawa alikiri kuwa Peter Orero ndiye mbunge wa sasa wa Kibra, Raila Junior alisema wakati umefika mahitaji ya wakazi yashughulikiwe na mtu anayefahamu eneo hilo vyema. “Siasa za Baba hazitakufa. Lazima tuwepo, na nimesema nimeanza safari,” akasema.

**23. ‘Mtoto wa Simba ni Simba’: Ida Endorses Raila Junior’s Kibra Bid**

NAIROBI, Kenya, Aug 24 – Mama Ida Odinga has handed her son, Raila Odinga Junior, to Kibra leaders and residents, urging them to guide him as he seeks to establish himself in elective politics and carry forward the political legacy of his late father, former Prime Minister Raila Odinga. Speaking during celebrations to mark her 76th birthday, Ida appeared to endorse her son's bid for the Kibra parliamentary seat while asking local leaders to mentor him and ensure he follows the right path. “Now you, Kibra residents, have come to me with other things. You are telling me that 'Mtoto wa simba ni simba.' If you have decided, then I don't have much to say, but I put him in your hands,” she said. Ida specifically appealed to Kibra leaders to take responsibility for guiding the younger Odinga as he seeks to enter elective politics. “To all of you here, I leave my child in your hands,” she said. “All of you who have come here at the front, I have given you my son. Take care of him, teach him and remind him. Many of you started with the former Prime Minister Raila Odinga in 1992. Show him the way so that he walks in the right way and does the right thing.” The remarks come barely weeks after Raila Junior publicly declared his intention to contest the Kibra parliamentary seat in the 2027 General Election. Raila Junior has in recent months intensified his grassroots activities in Kibra, a constituency that was a longstanding political stronghold of his late father. On August 3, he told a gathering of Kibra elders that he had already started planning for the constituency ahead of the 2027 elections, signalling that his interest in the seat had moved beyond speculation. “I am thinking about how to plan Kibra after 2027, while you are thinking about how to vote. So, I am already ahead of you. My concern now is how I am going to plan Kibra. Your concern will now be how I will get in that is not my problem.
