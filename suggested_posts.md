**2. Gachagua to Uhuru: We will deal with the government**

Democracy for Citizens Party (DCP) leader Rigathi Gachagua has urged retired President Uhuru Kenyatta to stay away from the affairs of the current government. Gachagua said the former Head of State should be allowed to enjoy his retirement without being drawn into political battles. Speaking during a church service in Nyeri on Sunday, Gachagua said he would oppose any attempt by President William Ruto's administration to enlist Uhuru's support. He praised the former president for maintaining that he has no interest in returning to active politics. “Please leave the former President Uhuru Kenyatta alone. We have asked Uhuru to stay at home and rest, and leave us to deal with the government,” Gachagua said. He added: “Uhuru decides to help the government; we shall disagree with him. And we do not want that.” Gachagua said Uhuru deserved to retire peacefully after serving as Kenya's president , warning against involving him in the political contests ahead of the 2027 General Election. “I want to commend Uhuru Kenyatta because he is steadfast and has maintained he does not want that. Uhuru must remain clean and be allowed to enjoy his retirement without anyone bothering him,” he said. His remarks came on the same day Uhuru rejected claims that he was interfering in current politics by supporting opposition figures. Speaking during the 23rd memorial service of former Vice-President Michael Wamalwa Kijana in Kitale, Trans Nzoia County, Uhuru said he had no interest in seeking any political position. “If you want votes, ask Kenyans politely; they will give them to you or deny you,” Uhuru said, adding that he was grateful for the opportunity Kenyans had given him to serve in leadership. The former president also called for peace, unity and respect as political parties begin positioning themselves for the 2027 elections. He cautioned Kenyans against allowing politicians to divide or intimidate them. “Kenyans are the ones who will decide whom they want,” Uhuru said.
