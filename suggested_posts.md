**1. Sifuna Goes after Ruto over President's Evidence on Uhuru Sponsoring Linda Mwananchi**

President Ruto was pressed in a newspaper interview to produce evidence that former president Uhuru Kenyatta is sponsoring Sifuna and his team. Nairobi senator Edwin Sifuna has scoffed at President William Ruto over his rhetoric that former president Uhuru Kenyatta is sponsoring Linda Mwananchi. In a Facebook statement on Sunday, August 23, Sifuna taunted Ruto for failing to substantiate his claims. Sifuna's remarks come after Ruto was pressed, in a newspaper interview, to provide evidence backing his repeated claims that Uhuru is funding opposition figures positioning themselves against him ahead of the 2027 general election. According to Sifuna, the president could not produce any proof beyond media reports. "That guy who supposedly holds all of Kenya's intellectual capital was asked a very simple question, to provide evidence that Uhuru is the sponsor of the opposition; and he said he only read about it in the newspaper... yet he claims we are the fools," Sifuna stated. The senator's comments add to weeks of escalating rhetoric between the two camps, after Ruto, speaking in Eldoret on Thursday, August 13, accused Uhuru of funding candidates positioning themselves to challenge his re-election. Ruto claimed his predecessor was driven by envy over achievements in sectors he said Uhuru had failed to deliver on during his own decade in office, including the Affordable Housing programme, Universal Health Coverage, and education funding. "When you look at their sponsor, didn't he fail to deliver on Affordable Housing, Universal Healthcare Coverage, and Education? Now he has gone to sponsor his protégés to come and compete with me. I will defeat them early in the morning because they have no plan, agenda, policies or vision," Ruto had stated. Ruto also dismissed calls from sections of the political class for him to serve only one term, terming such demands as rooted in tribalism, and maintained that his administration had distributed development equitably across all regions of the country, unlike previous governments.

**2. Detectives arrest suspect linked to murders of two elderly women in Trans Nzoia, Bungoma**

Detectives have arrested an 18-year-old man suspected of murdering an elderly woman in Trans Nzoia County and linked to the killing of another in Bungoma County. The first victim, identified as Florence Khosa, was admitted to Cheranganyi Nursing Home with serious injuries sustained during the attack and later succumbed to her injuries while undergoing treatment. The Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) said detectives launched a manhunt for the suspect, identified as Ezekiel Nyongesa Barasa, after he went into hiding. Detectives eventually traced and arrested Barasa, who was subjected to an identification parade where witnesses positively identified him. Further investigations have since linked the suspect to another alleged killing involving an 86-year-old woman in Tongaren, Bungoma County. Preliminary investigations have also established that Barasa may have sexually assaulted Khosa before her death. The DCI said detectives are conducting forensic examinations and pursuing other lines of inquiry to reconstruct the events and establish the circumstances surrounding the two alleged offences. The suspect remains in police custody as detectives piece together evidence ahead of his arraignment.
